# Meridian Finance Partners — Company Overview

> **Note:** Meridian Finance Partners, its clients (including Brightline Ventures), and every person named in this course are fictional. They exist so you can practice on realistic work without using your own company's confidential information. Any resemblance to real companies or people is coincidental.

## What We Do

Meridian Finance Partners is a finance services firm that becomes the finance function for growth-stage companies that need serious financial leadership but aren't yet ready — or aren't yet big enough — to bring on a full-time CFO.

Our clients are typically Series A through Series C startups and fast-growing SMBs. They're at an inflection point: investor expectations are rising, the business is scaling, and the financial complexity is outpacing what a bookkeeper or controller can handle on their own. That's where we come in.

We embed with client companies as their strategic finance partner. Depending on the engagement, that might mean owning their financial model, leading fundraising prep, preparing board materials, managing cash flow forecasting, or simply being the finance voice at the leadership table. Every engagement is different, but the mission is the same: give founders and operators the financial clarity they need to make confident decisions.

## Who We Serve

- **Stage:** Series A to Series C startups; revenue-generating SMBs scaling toward institutional investment
- **Industries:** SaaS, fintech, consumer, and marketplace businesses — sectors where growth metrics and unit economics matter
- **Typical needs:** Financial modeling, fundraising support, board reporting, cash management, FP&A buildout, finance team hiring and coaching

We typically work with a few dozen active client companies at any given time, with engagement depth ranging from a few hours per month to near-full-time embedded support.

## Company Stage and Size

Meridian is a growth-stage company itself — a lean team of roughly 25 people across the client-facing and operational sides of the business. The team is expanding: we're adding finance talent as client volume grows and building out the internal ops and marketing functions to support that growth.

We are not a big firm. Every person on the team has high ownership and wide scope. That means less bureaucracy and more impact, but it also means everyone carries meaningful admin overhead on top of their core work.

## Mission and Values

**Mission:** Give growth-stage companies access to CFO-level thinking — so founders can build with clarity, not guesswork.

**Values:**

- **Precision** — We work in finance. Accuracy is non-negotiable. We check our work, cite our sources, and don't guess when we can verify.
- **Clarity** — Our job is to take complexity and make it understandable. Whether it's a financial model or an internal email, if it's confusing, it's not done.
- **Trust** — Clients invite us into their most sensitive financial conversations. We earn and protect that trust every day.
- **Ownership** — Everyone at Meridian takes full responsibility for their work. We don't wait for permission and we don't make excuses.
- **Efficiency** — We're a lean team serving a high volume of clients. We build systems, not dependencies. We automate what can be automated and focus human effort where it creates the most value.

## How the Team Is Structured

Meridian operates with two distinct sides:

**Client-Facing Side**
Finance consultants who are directly embedded with client companies. Each consultant manages multiple client engagements simultaneously. They own deliverables like financial models, board decks, investor memos, and cash flow analyses. They communicate directly with client founders and finance teams.

**Operations, Admin, Marketing, and People Side**
The internal team that runs the business itself. This includes:
- Operations Coordinators who manage internal processes, vendor relationships, and the project tracker
- Executive and CoS support who handles scheduling, drafting, and cross-functional coordination
- Marketing who owns content, communications, and brand
- People and Culture who manages hiring, onboarding, and team health
- Admin who keeps the systems running

There is significant overlap between roles — this is a small team and everyone pitches in across functions when needed.

## Culture

**Remote-First**
The full team is distributed. There is no central office. We work across time zones and we've built our processes to be async by default. Decisions get documented in Notion. Updates go to Slack. Meetings are reserved for conversations that can't happen in writing.

**Async-Friendly**
We do not expect immediate responses outside of urgent situations. We batch communication, write clearly so that things don't require follow-up, and trust each other to manage our own time. Calendar blocking is a respected norm.

**High-Trust, High-Output**
We hire people who don't need to be managed closely. Expectations are clear, ownership is explicit, and autonomy is high. In exchange, we expect excellent work and proactive communication about blockers.

**Periodic In-Person**
A few times a year, the team gathers in person for planning sessions, team building, or off-sites. These are valued touchpoints in an otherwise fully remote environment.

**Small Team Energy**
With ~25 people, everyone knows everyone. There's very little politics and very little red tape. Good ideas get implemented quickly. Problems get surfaced and solved without needing to climb a reporting chain.
