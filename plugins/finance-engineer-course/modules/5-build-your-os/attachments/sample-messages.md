# Sample Sent Messages

> Use these if you don't have your own messages handy. They're written in one consistent voice — an operations coordinator at a company like Meridian — so Claude can study the patterns and capture a clear "How I Write" style. (When you do this for real, your own sent emails and Slack messages work best.)

---

**Email — to a vendor, following up on an overdue deliverable**

Hi Victor,

Quick follow-up on the onboarding portal access — we were expecting it last Friday and haven't seen it come through yet. Can you let me know where it stands and a realistic date? Happy to hop on a call if that's easier.

Thanks!
Jordan

---

**Slack — to the #ops channel, after a meeting**

Hey team — recap from the ops sync just now:

• Billing migration is on track for the 15th, Morgan owns it
• We're still waiting on the updated vendor list (Sam, end of week?)
• Parking the office-move conversation until next month

Shout if I missed anything.

---

**Email — to a colleague, declining a meeting request**

Hi Dana,

I don't think I need to be in the Thursday planning call — it looks like it's mostly design-side decisions and I'd just be a fly on the wall. Go ahead without me, and feel free to send me the notes after. I'll jump in if anything needs an ops hand.

Thanks!
Jordan

---

**Slack — quick check-in with a teammate**

Morning! Did the updated SOP ever make it into Notion? I want to point the new hire at it today. No rush if not — just let me know and I'll hold off.

---

**Email — to leadership, a short status note**

Hi Drew and Riley,

Short update on the Brightline onboarding: kickoff went well, the 90-day plan is in progress, and we're waiting on their financials (requested last week, still pending). One small risk — if those don't land by Friday, delivery slips a few days. I'll flag the moment it's at risk.

Thanks!
Jordan

---

**Slack — responding to a fire drill**

On it. Give me 20 minutes to pull the numbers together and I'll drop them here. If it's more urgent than that, ping me and I'll bump it to the top.

---

**Email — welcoming a new vendor contact**

Hi Lena,

Great to connect! I'll be your main point of contact on our side for anything operations-related. I've cc'd Morgan, who handles billing. Looking forward to working together — I'll send over the kickoff details by tomorrow.

Thanks!
Jordan

---

**Slack — closing the loop on an action item**

Done — the calendar invites are out and the agenda's linked in the event. One thing to flag: two people have a conflict at 2pm, so we may need to nudge it 30 minutes. I'll wait to hear back before changing anything.

---

**Email — a gentle nudge on a deadline**

Hi Sam,

No pressure, but checking in on the vendor list — we're hoping to finalize the budget by Friday and that's the last piece we're waiting on. Even a rough draft would unblock us. Let me know if you need anything from me to get it over the line.

Thanks!
Jordan

---

**Slack — a quick thank-you**

Just want to say thanks for jumping on that so fast earlier — it genuinely saved the day. Owe you a coffee. ☕
